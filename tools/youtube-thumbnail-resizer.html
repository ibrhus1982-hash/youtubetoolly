YouTube Thumbnail Resizer

YouTubeToolly's free YouTube Thumbnail Resizer makes it easy to resize your images for YouTube videos. Upload your image, choose a recommended size or enter custom dimensions, and download the resized thumbnail instantly.

The tool works directly in your browser, so you can resize thumbnails without installing image editing software.

What is the recommended YouTube thumbnail size?

A commonly recommended YouTube thumbnail size is 1280 × 720 pixels with a 16:9 aspect ratio. This gives creators a high-quality thumbnail suitable for YouTube video pages and different screen sizes.

Recommended YouTube Thumbnail
  • Width: 1280 pixels
  • Height: 720 pixels
  • Aspect ratio: 16:9
  • Common formats: JPG and PNG

How to Resize a YouTube Thumbnail

Resizing your thumbnail takes only a few simple steps:

  • Upload your JPG, PNG, or WebP image.
  • Choose a recommended thumbnail size.
  • Or enter your own custom width and height.
  • Choose JPG or PNG as the output format.
  • Click "Resize Thumbnail".
  • Preview and download your resized thumbnail.

Can I use a custom thumbnail size?

Yes. You can enter any width and height between 1 and 5000 pixels. If the aspect ratio option is enabled, the tool automatically maintains the original image ratio.

Does the tool work without uploading my image?

No. You need to select an image from your device before resizing it. The image is processed locally in your browser using JavaScript and the Canvas API.

Is the YouTube Thumbnail Resizer free?

Yes. YouTubeToolly's thumbnail resizer is free to use and does not require registration or software installation.

Frequently Asked Questions

What size should a YouTube thumbnail be?

1280 × 720 pixels with a 16:9 aspect ratio is a commonly recommended size for YouTube thumbnails.

Can I resize a thumbnail to 1280 × 720?

Yes. Select the 1280 × 720 preset and click "Resize Thumbnail".

Can I enter custom dimensions?

Yes. You can enter your preferred width and height using the custom dimension fields.

Can I keep the original aspect ratio?

Yes. Enable "Keep original aspect ratio" and the tool will automatically calculate the corresponding dimension.

Which image formats are supported?

The tool accepts JPG, PNG and WebP images and lets you download the result as JPG or PNG.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. The image is processed directly in your browser. The tool does not require your image to be uploaded to a server for resizing.

Does the tool work on mobile devices?

Yes. The YouTube Thumbnail Resizer works in modern mobile and desktop browsers.
