What is the recommended YouTube thumbnail size?

The commonly recommended YouTube thumbnail size is 1280 × 720 pixels, with a 16:9 aspect ratio. This size provides a high-quality image that works well across desktop computers, mobile devices, and other screens.

Recommended YouTube Thumbnail:
  • Width: 1280 pixels
  • Height: 720 pixels
  • Aspect ratio: 16:9
  • Format: JPG or PNG

Why resize a YouTube thumbnail?

A properly sized thumbnail can help your video look professional and consistent across different devices. Resizing an image also makes it easier to meet recommended dimensions before uploading it to YouTube.

  • Prepare images for YouTube videos.
  • Create thumbnails with a 16:9 layout.
  • Reduce unnecessarily large image dimensions.
  • Convert your thumbnail to JPG or PNG.
  • Preview the final image before downloading.
