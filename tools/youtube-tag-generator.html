YouTube Tag Generator

YouTubeToolly's YouTube Tag Generator helps creators quickly create a useful list of tags based on their video title, topic, and keywords.

Why Use YouTube Tags?

Tags can help describe the subject of a video and provide additional context about the content. Relevant tags are more useful than adding a large number of unrelated keywords.

How to Use the YouTube Tag Generator

  1. Enter your YouTube video title.
  2. Describe the main topic of your video.
  3. Add your most important keywords.
  4. Select the type of content.
  5. Click "Generate Tags".
  6. Review, edit, and copy the generated tags.

Tips for Better YouTube Tags

  • Use tags that accurately describe your video.
  • Include important words from your video topic.
  • Use specific phrases instead of only broad terms.
  • Consider common variations of your main topic.
  • Avoid unrelated or misleading keywords.
  • Review the generated tags before publishing your video.
Tip: Use your main topic and the most relevant search phrases rather than trying to fill your video with as many tags as possible.

Free and Browser-Based

YouTubeToolly generates tags directly in your browser. No external AI API is required and the information you enter is not uploaded to our server by this tool.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about YouTube tags.

What is a YouTube Tag Generator?

It is a tool that creates a list of relevant words and phrases that can be used as tags for a YouTube video.

How do I generate YouTube tags?

Enter your video title, topic, and keywords, choose your content type, and click Generate Tags.

Are these tags generated by AI?

No. This tool uses built-in browser-based templates and keyword combinations. It does not require an external AI API.

Can I edit the generated tags?

Yes. The generated tag list can be copied and edited before you add it to your YouTube video.

Should I use every generated tag?

Not necessarily. Choose the tags that are genuinely relevant to your video's subject and remove anything that does not fit.

Is the YouTube Tag Generator free?

Yes. YouTubeToolly's YouTube Tag Generator is free to use.

Does this tool save my video information?

No. The generator works directly in your browser and does not require you to upload your video or create an account.

Can I use this tool on mobile?

Yes. The tool is responsive and can be used on phones, tablets, and desktop computers.
