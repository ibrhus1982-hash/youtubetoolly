YouTube Tag Generator

YouTubeToolly's YouTube Tag Generator helps creators quickly create a useful list of tags based on their video title, topic, and keywords.

The generated tags are designed to help describe the subject of your video and give you relevant keyword ideas that you can review and edit before adding them to YouTube.

Why Use YouTube Tags?

YouTube tags can help describe the content and topic of a video. Relevant tags can provide additional context about what your video is about, especially when your topic can be described using different words or phrases.

The most important thing is relevance. Avoid adding unrelated keywords simply to increase the number of tags.

How to Use the YouTube Tag Generator

  • Enter your YouTube video title.
  • Describe the main topic of your video.
  • Add your most important keywords.
  • Select the type of content you are creating.
  • Click Generate Tags.
  • Review, edit and copy the generated tags.

Tips for Better YouTube Tags

  • Use tags that accurately describe your video.
  • Include important words naturally.
  • Use specific keyword phrases when appropriate.
  • Include useful variations of your main topic.
  • Avoid unrelated or misleading keywords.
  • Always review the generated tags before using them.
Tip: Choose relevant tags instead of trying to fill your tag list with as many keywords as possible.

Free and Browser-Based

This tool works directly in your browser and does not require an external AI API. Your video information is processed locally by the page and is not uploaded to a server by this tool.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about YouTube tags.

What is a YouTube Tag Generator?

A YouTube Tag Generator creates relevant keyword and phrase ideas that can be used as tags for a YouTube video.

How do I generate YouTube tags?

Enter your video title, topic and main keywords, choose your content type, and click Generate Tags.

Are these tags generated by AI?

No. This tool uses built-in keyword patterns and templates in your browser to generate tag suggestions.

Can I edit the generated tags?

Yes. The generated tags appear in an editable text box, allowing you to change or remove any tag before copying them.

Should I use every generated tag?

No. Only use tags that are relevant to your video and accurately describe its subject.

Is this YouTube Tag Generator free?

Yes. YouTubeToolly's YouTube Tag Generator is completely free to use.

Does the tool save my information?

No. The tool works directly in your browser and does not require you to create an account or submit your video information to a server.

Does the YouTube Tag Generator work on mobile?

Yes. The tool is responsive and works on phones, tablets, laptops and desktop computers.
