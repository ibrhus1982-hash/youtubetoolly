YouTube Earnings Calculator

YouTube earnings can vary significantly from one channel to another. This calculator provides a simple estimate based on your average daily views and RPM.

RPM represents the estimated revenue earned per 1,000 views after YouTube's share and other factors affecting creator revenue.

How to use the YouTube Earnings Calculator

  • Enter your average daily YouTube views.
  • Enter your estimated RPM.
  • Select your preferred currency.
  • Choose the number of days in your estimated month.
  • Click Calculate Earnings.

How are YouTube earnings calculated?

The basic estimate uses the following formula:

Estimated Earnings = Views ÷ 1,000 × RPM

For example, if a channel receives 10,000 daily views and has an RPM of $3, the estimated daily revenue would be approximately $30.

Why can actual earnings be different?

Actual earnings can change depending on many factors, including the percentage of monetized views, viewer location, advertiser demand, video category, ad formats, seasonality, and the type of content.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this YouTube Earnings Calculator free?

Yes. YouTubeToolly's earnings calculator is free to use and does not require an account.

What is RPM on YouTube?

RPM is a revenue metric that represents how much a creator earns per 1,000 views, based on the applicable YouTube revenue data.

Does 1,000 views always equal the same amount of money?

No. Revenue per 1,000 views can vary considerably depending on factors such as audience location, content category, advertiser demand, and monetized views.

Are these earnings guaranteed?

No. The calculator provides an estimate and actual earnings may be higher or lower.

Does the calculator send my information to a server?

No. The calculations are performed directly in your browser. Your entered values are not sent to an external service.
