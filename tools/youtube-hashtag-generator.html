YouTube Hashtag Generator

YouTubeToolly's YouTube Hashtag Generator helps creators quickly create a useful list of hashtags based on their video title, topic, and keywords.

The generated hashtags are designed to describe the subject of your video and give you relevant hashtag ideas that you can review and edit before using them on YouTube.

Why Use YouTube Hashtags?

YouTube hashtags can help viewers discover videos related to a particular topic. Relevant hashtags can also provide additional context about the subject of your video.

Relevance is important. Avoid adding unrelated hashtags simply to increase the number of hashtags in your video.

How to Use the YouTube Hashtag Generator

  • Enter your YouTube video title.
  • Describe the main topic of your video.
  • Add your most important keywords.
  • Select the type of content you are creating.
  • Click Generate Hashtags.
  • Review, edit and copy the generated hashtags.

Tips for Better YouTube Hashtags

  • Use hashtags that accurately describe your video.
  • Choose hashtags related to your main topic.
  • Use specific phrases when appropriate.
  • Include useful variations of your main topic.
  • Avoid unrelated or misleading hashtags.
  • Review the generated hashtags before using them.
Tip: Focus on relevant hashtags that clearly describe your video instead of using a large number of unrelated hashtags.

Free and Browser-Based

This tool works directly in your browser and does not require an external AI API. Your video information is processed locally by the page and is not uploaded to a server by this tool.
